# Cross-Check Prosedur Pengagihan Aset dengan Framework Profesional IFP & CFP di Malaysia

## Ringkasan

Dokumen ini melakukan kajian silang (cross-check) antara prosedur pengagihan aset dalam Enjin Pengagihan Aset projek EA-MY (faraid/wasiat/intestat berasaskan nilaian) dengan **dua framework profesional terakreditasi** di Malaysia: **Islamic Financial Planning (IFP)** — kelayakan *Islamic Financial Planner* di bawah IBFIM/FPAM — dan **Certified Financial Planner (CFP)** di bawah Financial Planning Association of Malaysia (FPAM). Tujuan: mengenal pasti komponen prosedur yang **SELARI** (boleh dijadikan lapisan pengesahan), komponen yang **TIDAK diliputi** IFP/CFP (statutori kita lebih terperinci), dan komponen yang **BERBEZA**. Kedudukan dokumen: IFP/CFP ialah **lapisan PENGESAHAN prosedur sahaja** — asas kuasa kekal **statutori** (Akta 97, Akta 98, Akta 532, NLC, CA 2016, dsb. seperti dipetakan dalam `03-processes/process-maps.md`).

## 1. Framework IFP (Islamic Financial Planning) di Malaysia

### 1.1 Definisi dan kedudukan kelayakan IFP

*Islamic Financial Planner (IFP)* ditakrifkan IBFIM sebagai "a professional qualification programme designed for professionals and executives that serves the retail segment of the Islamic financial market", dan "a joint collaboration between IBFIM and Financial Planning Association of Malaysia (FPAM)". IBFIM menyatakan IFP "is pre-requisite for Capital Market Services Representative's License (CMSRL) by Securities Commission (SC) and Islamic Financial Advisers' Representative License (FAR) by Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) to carry out the regulated activity of financial planning" [Sumber: IBFIM, IFP – Products and Services 2026 — https://info.ibfimonline.com/ifp/, dicapai 2026-11-01].

Takrifan akademik: "Islamic financial planning is a step-by-step process of managing the wealth of Muslims" — iaitu proses menentukan bagaimana individu Muslim mencapai matlamat hidupnya melalui pengurusan kewangan selaras dengan Shariah [Sumber: Omar & Khairi, "Islamic Financial Planning: Towards Achieving Al-Falah", Journal of Islamic, Social and Finance (JIPSF), UiTM, 2024 — https://journal.uitm.edu.my/index.php/JIPSF/article/download/3568/1701/12636, dicapai 2026-11-01; Sumber: Yusoff, Engku Ali & Zakariyah, "Islamic Financial Planning: Towards Sustaining the Financial Wellbeing of Muslim Families in Malaysia Post Covid-19", Turkish Journal of Islamic Economics (TUJISE) 8(Special Issue) 2021, hlm. 355–376, DOI 10.26414/A2380 — https://tujise.org/issues/special-issue-06-2021/a2380, dicapai 2026-11-01].

### 1.2 Badan pengakreditasi dan silibus (IBFIM)

Silibus IFP "covers six modules covering all aspects of financial planning, similar to the CFP, but will be in full compliance with Shariah principles and Islamic tenets" — enam modul peperiksaan: (1) Fundamentals of Islamic Financial Planning; (2) Risk Management and Takaful Planning; (3) Islamic Investment Planning; (4) Zakat and Tax Planning; (5) **Islamic Estate, Retirement and Waqf Planning**; (6) Financial Plan Construction and Professional Responsibilities [Sumber: Islamic Banking and Finance Institute Malaysia (brochure) — https://www.scribd.com/document/74785448/Islamic-Banking-and-Finance-Institute-Malaysia, dicapai 2026-11-01]. Struktur 2016 bagi *Challenge Status* FPAM menyenaraikan M1 Shariah & Major Components of Islamic Financial Planning; **M2 Islamic Risk Management, Estate & Waqf Planning**; M3 Islamic Investment, Retirement, Zakat & Tax Planning; M4 Islamic Financial Plan Construction & Professional Responsibilities [Sumber: FPAM, IFP 2016 trade-member form — https://www.fpam.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/IFP-2016_trade-member-form.pdf, dicapai 2026-11-01]. Struktur Challenge Status semasa (2026) ialah satu modul "CS: Shariah, Islamic Financial Plan Construction and Professional Responsibilities" (42 jam; Pensyarah/kemas kini harga RM7,711.20 dalam talian) [Sumber: IBFIM, IFP – Products and Services 2026 — https://info.ibfimonline.com/ifp/, dicapai 2026-11-01]. Syarat kemasukan Challenge Status: pemegang CFP; pemegang kelayakan profesional lain (CFA, CA) dengan 3 tahun pengalaman; atau ijazah + 5 tahun pengalaman industri [Sumber: IBFIM, Programme Book — https://ibfimonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/IBFIM-PM-Book-Final-email-compressed.pdf, dicapai 2026-11-01; IBFIM, capaian sama 2026].

Pengesahan pengawal selia: BNM mengiktiraf kelayakan **Islamic FAR** melalui "Shariah RFP conferred by the MFPC; or **Islamic Financial Planner conferred by the FPAM**", dengan *mandatory knowledge areas* "foundation of Islamic financial planning, risk management, takaful planning and Shariah investment planning"; manakala FAR (konvensional) melalui "Certified Financial Planner (CFP) conferred by the Financial Planning Association of Malaysia (FPAM)" atau RFP (MFPC) [Sumber: BNM, FAQ Financial Advisory (Mac 2022) — https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/855632/FAQ-Financial-Advisory-Mar2022.pdf, dicapai 2026-11-01]. IBFIM turut menerbitkan teks rujukan estet Islam: Azman Ismail, *Islamic Inheritance Planning 101* (IBFIM, 2013) [Sumber: Abdul Rahman & Hassan, "Islamic Estate Planning in Malaysia: The Dilemma", Journal of Islam in Asia 17(2) 2020 — https://irep.iium.edu.my/84585/1/84585_Islamic%20Estate%20Planning%20in%20Malaysia.pdf, dicapai 2026-11-01].

*Hasil pembelajaran* (learning outcomes) IFP yang berkaitan terus dengan prosedur kami: pelajar mampu "Realise the steps and requirement in Islamic financial planning process" dan "Formulate and execute **implementation and review process** of the plan as an ongoing advisory service" [Sumber: IBFIM, IFP – Products and Services 2026 — https://info.ibfimonline.com/ifp/, dicapai 2026-11-01].

### 1.3 Langkah-langkah proses perancangan harta Islam (sebagaimana sumber)

**(i) Proses 6-langkah EGADIM (IFP menerima pakai standard FPSB).** "Islamic financial planning adopts the six-steps process from the global standard practice of financial planning profession established by Financial Planning Standards Board (FPSB). The practice standard is adopted by all financial planning bodies around the world, including the FPAM and MFPC in Malaysia. It is known with an acronym **EGADIM**. It starts with **establishing client-planner relationship, gathering financial and non-financial data, analysing the data, developing financial plans, implementing the plans and finally monitoring and reviewing the plans** to ensure that they are on the right track to achieve the objectives." Proses FPSB enam langkah ini "is adopted as a basis for a modified six-step process for Islamic financial planning" dengan falsafah/konsep Islam diserapkan pada setiap langkah [Sumber: Yusoff, Engku Ali & Zakariyah, TUJISE 2021, capaian sama seperti di atas]. Proses enam langkah yang sama dinyatakan dalam ISO 22222:2005 (establish relationship → gather data & goals → analyse & evaluate → develop & present → implement → monitor) [Sumber: Wikipedia, "Financial planner" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_planner, dicapai 2026-11-01].

**(ii) Lima komponen IFP** (diadaptasi daripada IBFIM 2018): "**wealth generation, wealth accumulation, wealth protection, wealth distribution and wealth purification**" [Sumber: Omar & Khairi, JIPSF 2024, capaian sama seperti di atas; Sumber: Yusoff et al., TUJISE 2021 — "the generation of wealth, purification of wealth, protection of wealth, accumulation of wealth, and distribution of wealth"]. Komponen *wealth distribution* melalui estet mempunyai tiga instrumen utama (mensabitkan Kamarudin & Muhamad 2018): "**Hibah**: A gift that refers to a voluntary transfer of ownership from one person to another; **Wasiyyah**: A contract that is made during the lifetime of testators to distribute their estate to beneficiaries which is effective only after their death; **Faraid**: The distribution of the deceased's assets to their heirs following the portions as set out in Al-Quran" [Sumber: Omar & Khairi, JIPSF 2024, capaian sama].

**(iii) Tiga kaedah perancangan estet individu** (Kamarudin et al.): "Option 1 — Planning method during lifetime — Hibah/ Waqf/ Wasiyyah; Option 2 — Left to heirs — Faraid; Option 3 — Appointment of third-party during lifetime — Wasiyyah/ Trust" [Sumber: Abdul Rahman & Hassan, Journal of Islam in Asia 2020 (memetik Kamarudin et al.), capaian sama; asal: Kamarudin & Muhamad, "Islamic Estate Planning and Management: Malaysian experience" (UTM, 2018) — http://eprints.utm.my/82093/1/MohdKhairyKamarudin2018_%20IslamicEstatePlanningandManagementMalaysian.pdf (teks penuh gagal dicapai — PERLU SAHKAN)]. Kajian UTM yang sama menyatakan pengagihan estet si mati terbahagi kepada enam komponen: "wealth that has been gifted, trusted or endowed during lifetime, funeral expenses, payment of debt to Allah and mankind, wasiyyah distribution, matrimonial wealth claiming and faraid distribution" — iaitu harta hibah/amanah/waqf diasingkan dahulu, diikuti belanja pengkebumian, hutang (termasuk zakat tertunggak), wasiyyah, tuntutan harta sepencarian, akhirnya faraid [Sumber: Kamarudin & Muhamad 2018, capaian sama seperti di atas (PERLU SAHKAN teks penuh)].

**(iv) Lima langkah pentadbiran pusaka Islam selepas kematian** (UiTM): "(1) The first step in managing a Muslim inheritance involves **organising the assets** that the deceased owned or managed during their lifetime" (termasuk mengasingkan harta amanah, hadiah dan wasiat yang bukan milik eksklusif si mati); "(2) **Repaying the debts** left by the deceased is the next step in administering a Muslim estate" — hutang dunia dan hutang akhirat (cth. haji tertunas); "(3) The distribution of **marital property** is the third phase of estate management" (harta sepencarian); "(4) **Implementing the deceased's last will** is the fourth stage in Islamic estate administration … as long as it does not exceed one-third of the remaining estate"; "(5) **Executing faraid** on the bequest allocated by the deceased to the legitimate heirs is the fifth and final step in administering Islamic inheritance" [Sumber: Mohd Yusof & Saiman, "The Level of Understanding on the Importance of Estate Management Among the Muslim Community in Selangor", Journal of Contemporary Islamic Studies (UiTM) 10(2) 2024 — https://ir.uitm.edu.my/id/eprint/111182/1/111182.pdf, dicapai 2026-11-01]. Kertas yang sama turut merujuk Yaakob et al. (2020): pentadbiran harta merangkumi "seven distinct processes — five are life planning measures the property owner takes, while the other two are estate management steps taken by the deceased's heirs" [Sumber: Mohd Yusof & Saiman 2024, capaian sama (sitasi sekunder — PERLU SAHKAN teks asal Yaakob et al. 2020)].

**(v) Proses pra-pentadbiran ARB** (sumber korporat): "What must be completed prior to the commencement of the estate administration process? — **Identify the assets and liabilities** left by the deceased; **Determine whether the deceased left a will, hibah (gift), trust, or nomination**; **Collect all relevant documents** related to beneficiaries, assets, liabilities, and other pertinent paperwork; **Apply for the administration** of the deceased's estate at ARB if there are assets to be administered" [Sumber: AmanahRaya, Estate Administration — https://www.amanahraya.my/services/estate-administration/, dicapai 2026-11-01]. Dalam amalan, "Amanah Raya Berhad performs five tasks: representing the beneficiaries in court and managing the heirs' estate; **reviewing the list of assets and liabilities and identifying the deceased person's creditors and debtors**; **paying off all the deceased person's debts**; **issuing powers of attorney**; and **distributing inheritance in accordance with faraid law for Muslims and the Distribution Act of 1958** for non-Muslims" [Sumber: Saiman & Romeyzee, "Unclaimed Money in Islamic Inheritance: A Case Study at Amanah Raya Berhad", Sains Insani (USIM) 2024 — https://sainsinsani.usim.edu.my/index.php/sainsinsani/article/download/648/339/4225, dicapai 2026-11-01]. Khidmat penulisan wasiat ARB melalui MyGOV merangkumi "listing assets, appointing beneficiaries, naming executors, and including specific inheritance instructions" (tempoh 14 hari bekerja) [Sumber: MyGOV, Application for Will Writing — https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/digital-services/permohonan-penulisan-wasiat, dicapai 2026-11-01]. Bagi hibah, ARB melaksanakan proses 4 peringkat: donor menjadi *first trustee*, ARB *second trustee*; kelulusan pemberi pinjaman; tiga *trust deed* (SDH, SADH, SIAH); pengesahan Mahkamah Tinggi Syariah semasa hidup donor + *Comprehensive Will* [Sumber: IIUM, "Transformation of Collateral Hibah in Amanah Raya Berhad", IIUM Law Journal — https://journals.iium.edu.my/iiumlj/index.php/iiumlj/article/download/953/469/3402, dicapai 2026-11-01].

**(vi) Aliran pentadbiran pusaka Islam Malaysia** (kertas IIUM): "The procedure begins with the discovery of either the deceased dies **intestate or testate** … the main authorities that are involved in settling Islamic estate administration and distribution are the **Department of Director General of Lands and Mines [JKPTG], the Syariah Court and High Court**" — dengan *Faraid Certificate from the Syariah Court* sebagai prasyarat pada semua saluran (<RM600,000 intestat melalui petisyen JKPTG; >RM600,000 melalui *Letter of Administration* dengan dua penjamin atau *Grant of Probate* di Mahkamah Tinggi; pengagihan mengikut faraid) [Sumber: Abdul Rahman & Hassan, Journal of Islam in Asia 17(2) 2020, capaian sama seperti di atas]. **Nota kritikal:** ambang RM600,000 dalam aliran kertas 2020 ini bercanggah dengan had pusaka kecil semasa **RM5 juta** (Act A1643, berkuat kuasa 15 Julai 2024) dalam Akta 98 — sumber akademik ketinggalan zaman; statut kekal rujukan utama [Rujukan dalaman: 03-processes/process-maps.md, laluan (b)].

## 2. Framework CFP (FPAM Malaysia)

### 2.1 Struktur pensijilan CFP dan modul estate planning

Pensijilan CFP (FPAM) terkini terdiri daripada lima modul: Module 1 – Foundation in Financial Planning with Behavioural Psychology of Financial Planning; **Module 2 – Insurance and Risk Management with Estate Planning**; Module 3 – Investment Planning and Portfolio Management; Module 4 – Retirement Planning & Tax Planning; Module 5 – Financial Plan Construction and Professional Responsibilities [Sumber: FPAM, Be a CFP Professional — https://www.fpam.org.my/membership/be-a-cfp-professional/, dicapai 2026-11-01]. Pemegang IFP diiktiraf FPAM untuk *Challenge Status* (pengecualian Module 1–4) bersama ChFC, CFA, RFP dan kelayakan lain dengan 3 tahun pengalaman kewangan peribadi (termasuk "estate planning, banking, taxation, trusts, retirement planning") [Sumber: FPAM, capaian sama]. Peperiksaan Module 5 Paper 2 mengkehendaki calon "**Prepare a Financial Plan based on case study**" [Sumber: FPAM, capaian sama].

### 2.2 Proses perancangan kewangan enam langkah (6-step financial planning process)

Standard amalan yang diterima pakai oleh FPAM (dan MFPC) ialah proses enam langkah FPSB (EGADIM, lihat §1.3(i)) [Sumber: Yusoff et al., TUJISE 2021, capaian sama seperti di atas]. Bagi perbandingan, *RFP Practice Standards* (MFPC) memperincikan enam langkah: "Step 1 Setting Goals, Objectives & Priorities; Step 2 Gathering Relevant Data & Information; Step 3 Analyzing Information & Assessing Financial Status; Step 4 Developing and Presenting a Financial Plan for Implementation; Step 5 Executing the Financial Plan; Step 6 Monitoring Execution & Reviewing of the Financial Plan" [Sumber: MFPC, RFP Programme Module 1 Chapter 1: The Financial Planning Environment, Phenomenon and Process — https://erfprmaterialsm1.weebly.com/uploads/8/4/3/6/84366182/m1-c1.pdf, dicapai 2026-11-01]. Silibus M1 FPAM (2015) memperuntukkan topik "The estate planning process: Definition of estate planning; Role of an estate planner; Techniques for preserving wealth; Role of life insurance in estate planning; Types of trusts; Taxation of trusts; Wills; **Syariah Law in relation to estate planning**" [Sumber: FPAM, Module 1 Foundation in Financial Planning and Tax Planning Syllabus (2015) — https://www.fpam.org.my/main/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/M1-Foundation-in-Financial-Planning-and-Tax-Planning-Syllabus-Final.pdf, dicapai 2026-11-01].

### 2.3 Modul estate planning dalam silibus CFP (Module 2) — petikan topik

Objektif kursus M2: calon memahami "estate planning **tools such will, power of attorney and trust** as well as taxation issues affecting estate planning" dan dapat "formulate appropriate financial plans in assisting their clients" [Sumber: FPAM, Syllabus Topics Module 2 Insurance Planning and Estate Planning (2025) — https://fpam.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Syllabus-Topics-Module-2-Insurance-Planning-and-Estate-Planning.pdf, dicapai 2026-11-01]. Topik estet (sebagaimana silibus):

- **Topic 12: Estate Planning Fundamentals** — "What is estate planning; Estate planning purposes and objectives; What is an estate; The players in estate planning; **The process involved in estate planning**; Strategies of protection, preservation and distribution of wealth; Overview of estate planning instruments commonly used; Taxation implications of estate planning."
- **Topic 13: Wills and Will Planning** — "The definition of a Will; Types of Wills; Law of testacy and intestacy: Distribution Act 1997 [*sic* — Akta Pembahagian 1958 (Act 300), cetakan semula 1997], Probate and Administration Act 1959, Wills Act 1959, **Islamic law of inheritance**; Planning considerations in Will planning; Steps and legal requirements in Will planning; **The probate and administration process**; Will planning for business owners."
- **Topic 14: Trusts** — klasifikasi amanah (express/implied/statutory; fixed vs discretionary; living vs testamentary; revocable vs irrevocable), "Application of trust instruments in estate planning and advantages of a trust", hak benefisiari.
- **Topic 15: Powers of Attorney** — Akta Kuasa Kuasa 1949, aspek prosedural, tugas donee ("comes in useful in handling the affairs of the incapacitated and disabled individuals").
- **Topic 16: Duties and Power of the Personal Representative** — "**Estate administration**; Duties of a Personal Representative; Powers of a Personal Representative or Trustee; Investment management of trust assets" (termasuk ujian *prudent person*).
- **Topic 17: Special Estate Planning Issues for Business Owners** — peneruskan perniagaan (buy-sell), penilaian perniagaan, pilihan pembiayaan.

[Sumber: FPAM, Syllabus Topics Module 2 (2025), capaian sama seperti di atas.] Topik insurans M2 (Topic 4–5) memasukkan "How much of life insurance is needed — **settlement of debts and liabilities; maintenance of dependents** and the 'Human Life Value' Concept" dan *trust policies* — iaitu likuiditi estet melalui insurans [Sumber: FPAM, capaian sama].

### 2.4 Prosedur pentadbiran estet dalam amalan perancang kewangan

Berdasarkan silibus M2, perancang kewangan CFP terlatih untuk: (a) merancang wasiat termasuk "steps and legal requirements in Will planning" dan "the probate and administration process"; (b) melaksanakan pentadbiran estet melalui *personal representative* — kutip aset, jelaskan liabiliti, urus pemindahan; (c) menasihati instrumen amanah dan kuasa kuasa bagi klien kurang upaya; (d) menangani isu khas pemilik perniagaan [Sumber: FPAM, Syllabus Topics Module 2 (2025), capaian sama]. Subjek "Syariah Law in relation to estate planning" wujud sebagai satu topik dalam silibus M1 dan "Islamic law of inheritance" sebagai sebahagian topik wasiat M2 — liputan Shariah dalam CFP adalah **sebahagian (subset)**, bukan terpasak merentas semua modul sebagaimana IFP [Sumber: FPAM M1 Syllabus 2015 dan M2 Syllabus 2025, capaian sama seperti di atas].

## 4. Analisis

### 4.1 Komponen SELARI — boleh dijadikan lapisan PENGESAHAN

1. **Inventori aset & liabiliti sebagai langkah pertama.** IFP (EGADIM-G; langkah pertama pentadbiran UiTM; pra-pentadbiran ARB "identify the assets and liabilities") dan CFP (fact-finding Langkah 2) sejajar dengan langkah 1 semua laluan statutori kita (kumpul sijil kematian, wasiat, senarai aset/liabiliti/waris) [Sumber: TUJISE 2021; Mohd Yusof & Saiman 2024; AmanahRaya, capaian sama; Rujukan dalaman: 03-processes/process-maps.md].
2. **Penjelasan hutang SEBELUM pengagihan.** Susunan fiqh IFP (hutang dunia + akhirat dibayar sebelum wasiat/faraid) selari dengan tugas pentadbir PAA "kumpul aset, jelaskan hutang, agihkan" dan 5 tugas ARB (bayar hutang sebelum agih) [Sumber: Mohd Yusof & Saiman 2024; Saiman & Romeyzee 2024; Rujukan dalaman: process-maps laluan (a) langkah 8].
3. **Urutan pengagihan fiqh: pengkebumian → hutang → wasiat (≤1/3) → sepencarian → faraid.** Enam komponen UTM dan 5 langkah UiTM memberikan kerangka turutan yang boleh mengesahkan turutan pengiraan Enjin Pengagihan Aset [Sumber: Kamarudin & Muhamad 2018; Mohd Yusof & Saiman 2024, capaian sama].
4. **Sijil Faraid sebagai prasyarat saluran Muslim.** Aliran IIUM (*Faraid Certificate from the Syariah Court* pada semua saluran) mengesahkan keperluan s.50 Akta 505 dalam peta proses kita [Sumber: Abdul Rahman & Hassan 2020; Rujukan dalaman: process-maps, peta keputusan #2].
5. **Instrumen semasa hidup: wasiat/hibah/waqf/amanah/penamaan.** 3 kaedah Kamarudin + pra-ARB ("determine whether the deceased left a will, hibah, trust, or nomination") selari dengan percabangan wasiat→probat / tiada wasiat→SKM dan laluan penamaan KWSP/takaful kita [Sumber: Abdul Rahman & Hassan 2020; AmanahRaya, capaian sama].
6. **Tugas pentadbir.** Topic 16 CFP (*duties and powers of the personal representative*) dan 5 tugas ARB mengesahkan peranan pentadbir statutori kita (bon, senarai aset Arahan Amalan 3/2015, komisen ≤5%) [Sumber: FPAM M2 Syllabus 2025; Saiman & Romeyzee 2024, capaian sama; Rujukan dalaman: process-maps laluan (a) langkah 4/7/8].
7. **Kelengkapan dokumen sebagai faktor kelancaran.** "Collect all relevant documents" (ARB) & fact-finding (CFP/IFP) mengesahkan nota kita "dokumen lengkap ialah faktor terbesar mengelak kelewatan" [Sumber: AmanahRaya, capaian sama; Rujukan dalaman: process-maps, prinsip umum].
8. **Kaedah alternatif muafakat.** Amalan "consensus" (= takharuj) di Unit Pembahagian Pusaka selari dengan pilihan PPKP "faraid / Akta 300 / muafakat" [Sumber: Mohd Yusof & Saiman 2024, capaian sama; Rujukan dalaman: process-maps laluan (b) langkah 4].

### 4.2 Komponen yang IFP/CFP TIDAK liputi — statutori kita lebih terperinci

Framework profesional beroperasi pada aras **nasihat/prinsip**; ia tidak memuatkan butir mekanikal statutori yang menjadi teras peta proses kita:

- Tatacara **MyLAND** (Borang A s.8 Akta 98, tandatangan di hadapan Majistrej/Pesuruhjaya Sumpah, DDA, mesyuarat waris Pentadbir Tanah, fi P.U.(A) 194/2024: RM30/RM50/0.2%/0.3%, MyLAND ePayment) — tiada dalam silibus IFP/CFP yang diperoleh.
- **Bon pentadbiran dua penjamin** (PAA s.35, pengecualian ≤RM50,000 dan *trust corporation*), fi pemfailan RM160/RM16 warta, Arahan Amalan 3/2009 (30 hari) dan 3/2015.
- **eGUMIS/WTD** (Borang UMA-7, AM 80, ambang 2/7 tahun, pindaan Akta 370) — sebutan "unclaimed money" wujud dalam kajian IFP secara isu, bukan prosedur.
- **NLC** s.346 pendaftaran "as representative", s.346(5) larangan, Borang 14A, duti setem LHDN/JPPH, s.166(1)(d) geran hilang, s.433B waris bukan warganegara — CFP merujuk "probate and administration process" secara umum sahaja.
- **CA 2016 s.109** transmission syer ≤60 hari, s.105(5), resealing geran asing.
- **Dwi-jurisdiction** (Senarai Persekutuan 4(e)(i) vs Senarai Negeri II Item 1; twin-track *Latifah*; perintah syariah tidak boleh daftar di pejabat tanah) — silibus M2 CFP hanya menyebut "Islamic law of inheritance" tanpa mekanisma dwi-jurisdiction; kertas IIUM menyentuh autoriti tetapi bukan doktrin konflik.
- **Had RM5 juta** pusaka kecil (Act A1643/2024) — kertas akademik IFP (IIUM 2020) masih guna RM600,000 yang usang — bukti statutori kita lebih semasakini.

### 4.3 Komponen BERBEZA

1. **Orientasi masa:** IFP/CFP = perancangan **sebelum kematian** (advisory, hubungan klien-planner, semakan berkala); proses statutori kita = **pentadbiran selepas kematian**. EGADIM tidak mempunyai fasa analogi pasca-kematian penuh — ia berhenti dengan "monitor & review" pelan hidup.
2. **Faraid sebagai mandatori vs pilihan saluran:** IFP meletakkan faraid sebagai langkah terakhir mandatur fiqh; statutori kita memberi waris pilihan kaedah (faraid/Akta 300/muafakat) di PPKP; CFP memperlakukan hukum pusaka Islam sebagai subset topik wasiat.
3. **Skop Shariah:** IFP memasukkan Shariah merentas semua modul (zakat, takaful, waqf, wasiyyah); CFP konvensional dengan topik Syariah terhad; proses statutori kita agama-neutral dari segi saluran (dokumen faraid sebagai input).
4. **Nilai (valuation):** CFP mengajar penilaian perniagaan & keperluan insurans (*Human Life Value*); statutori kita menilai aset untuk duti setem/LHDN dan had saluran — matlamat penilaian berbeza (perancangan vs percukaian/saluran).

## 5. Kesimpulan

### 5.1 Komponen prosedur pengagihan yang DISAHKAN oleh framework profesional

Boleh dicatat dalam dokumen webapp sebagai "disahkan silang oleh IFP/CFP" (pengesahan prosedur, bukan kuasa):

1. **Inventori aset & liabiliti dahulu** (IFP EGADIM-G; CFP Step 2; ARB; UiTM) — selari langkah 1 laluan kita.
2. **Penjelasan hutang sebelum pengagihan** (UTM/UiTM; CFP Topic 16; PAA; ARB).
3. **Turutan fiqh pengagihan**: asing harta hibah/amanah/waqf → belanja pengkebumian → hutang → wasiat ≤1/3 → harta sepencarian → faraid (UTM enam komponen; UiTM 5 langkah) — mengesahkan turutan kiraan Enjin Faraid.
4. **Sijil Faraid prasyarat saluran Muslim** (IIUM; Akta 505 s.50 dalam peta kita).
5. **Instrumen semasa hidup** wasiat/hibah/wasiyyah/amanah/penamaan menentukan saluran (Kamarudin 3 kaedah; ARB; CFP Topic 13–14).
6. **Tugas pentadbir** (kutip, jelaskan, agih, pemindahan) — CFP Topic 16 = ARB 5 tugas = PAA.
7. **Penama Muslim = wasi** bagi KWSP/takaful (fatwa MKI dalam laluan (e) kita; IFP takaful module sejajar).
8. **Kelengkapan dokumen = faktor kelancaran utama.**
9. **Proses 6-langkah EGADIM sebagai pembalut (wrapper)** bagi modul perancangan-pendapatan-pengguna di webapp sebelum fasa pusaka.

### 5.2 Komponen yang hanya berasaskan statutori (tidak memerlukan pengesahan IFP/CFP)

- Dokumen & fi setiap saluran: Borang A/DDA/MyLAND/ePayment PPKP; petisyen e-Kehakiman (Kod 31/32), fi RM160/RM16; bon penjamin s.35 PAA; UMA-7/AM 80/UMA-8S (eGUMIS/JANM); Borang 14A + duti setem LHDN (NLC); transmission s.109 CA 2016; fi ARB PTCA.
- Ambang kuasa: RM5 juta (Akta 98, Act A1643/2024); RM600,000 (ARB s.17(1) PTCA); 2/7 tahun (Akta 370); umur 100 KWSP.
- Dwi-jurisdiction & doktrin *Latifah* twin-track; Surat Edaran Ketua Pengarah Tanah & Galian (perintah syariah tidak boleh daftar).
- Tempoh statutori: 30 hari pendengaran (AA 3/2009); 60 hari transmission syer; 6 bulan bantuan kematian KWSP.

### 5.3 Komponen yang IFP/CFP ada tetapi kita belum ada (jurang untuk dipertimbang)

1. **Langkah harta sepencarian** (marital property) sebelum faraid (UiTM langkah 3; UTM) — peta proses kita tiada langkah tuntutan sepencarian di Mahkamah Syariah; tambah sebagai pintu semakan dalam aliran kes.
2. **Zakat/hutang akhirat** (haji, zakat tertunggak) dalam turutan penjelasan kewajipan (UTM; komponen *wealth purification* IFP) — Enjin fi perlu kemas kini kategori "hutang Allah" dalam lejar pusaka sebelum faraid.
3. **Loop monitoring & review pasca-pengagihan** (EGADIM-M; CFP Step 6) — peta proses berakhir di pemindahan; pertimbang peringkat semakan tertutup (cth. pengesahan pendaftaran/pembayaran selesai, rekonsiliasi lejar) — selari juga dengan gate S9 rekonsiliasi dalam `modul-pematuhan-enjin-fi.md`.
4. **Perancangan kekurangan upaya** (CFP Topic 15 Powers of Attorney) — di luar skop semasa webapp; nyatakan sebagai batasan produk.
5. **Penerusuan perniagaan pemilik syer** (CFP Topic 17 buy-sell/penilaian) — laluan (g) hanya urus transmission; pertimbang nota keupayaan.
6. **Kedalaman instrumen amanah (trust)** — CFP Topic 14 (living/testamentary/revocable; hak benefisiari) melampaui skop ARB; penting bagi waris bawah umur.
7. **Analisis keperluan likuiditi estet** (CFP M2 Topic 4 "settlement of debts & liabilities" melalui insurans/takaful) — modul nasihat webapp boleh pra-skrin sama ada harta tunai/likuiditi mencukupi bagi hutang & belanja pentadbiran.
8. **Standard penglibatan klien & kod etika perancang** (IFP/CFP Professional Responsibilities; BNM FAQ FA) — model pasangan agent-human kita (`konsep-model-agent-human.md`) sedia ada pintu stage-gate; boleh rujuk kod etika FPAM/IBFIM sebagai benchmark tatakelila.

## Jurang maklumat (Gaps)

1. **Silibus penuh modul IFP "Islamic Estate, Retirement and Waqf Planning" tidak diterbitkan secara awam** — kandungan diketahui melalui brosur/party ketiga sahaja (PERLU SAHKAN dengan panduan modul rasmi IBFIM atau teks Azman Ismail, *Islamic Inheritance Planning 101*, IBFIM 2013 — dirujuk dalam kertas IIUM tetapi buku teks tidak dicapai dalam kajian ini).
2. **Struktur IFP berubah mengikut masa** (6 modul *full-fledged* → 4 modul 2016 FPAM → Challenge Status tunggal "CS" 2026) — evolusi tidak didokumenkan dalam satu sumber rasmi; pelbagai varian beredar (PERLU SAHKAN struktur semasa terus dengan IBFIM).
3. **Tiada garis panduan khusus BNM/SC bagi "prosedur perancangan estet Islam"** — capaian hanya menemui peraturan pelesenan peramal kewangan (FAQ Financial Advisory BNM Mac 2022; CMSRL SC). Kertas IIUM (2020) sendiri menyatakan industri IEP mengalami "improper regulation… absence of standard practices" dan mencadangkan *Shariah advisory framework* untuk institusi IEP — status pelaksanaan cadangan itu tidak disahkan (PERLU SAHKAN).
4. **Kertas UTM Kamarudin & Muhamad (2018) gagal diekstrak sepenuhnya** (halaman memblok capaian automatik) — butir 6 komponen pengagihan diperoleh melalui petikan enjin carian + sitasi sekunder (JIPSF 2024; IIUM 2020) (PERLU SAHKAN teks penuh).
5. **Aliran RM600,000 bagi laluan JKPTG dalam kertas IIUM (2020) bercanggah dengan had RM5 juta semasa** (Act A1643, kuat kuasa 15 Julai 2024) — sumber akademik usang; mengesahkan lagi bahawa statut mesti kekal asas kuasa, framework akademik hanya lapisan pengesahan.
6. **Kertas Yaakob et al. (2020)** — "seven distinct processes (5 semasa hidup + 2 selepas kematian)" hanya diperoleh sebagai sitasi sekunder dalam kertas UiTM JCIS (PERLU SAHKAN teks asal: "The Importance of Administration of Estates in Malaysia from Legal and Maqasid Syariah Perspectives", INSLA e-Proceeding, hlm. 689–699).
7. **Angka 700,000 deklarasi wasiyyah** (IIUM 2020, berbanding ~30 juta rakyat) — angka 2020; perlu angka semasa daripada ARB/JKPTG (PERLU SAHKAN).
8. **Kandungan terperinci FPAM Module 5 (Financial Plan Construction and Professional Responsibilities)** — hanya topik garis lusan diperoleh; butir standard profesional/etika untuk amalan pentadbiran estet tidak diekstrak (PERLU SAHKAN silibus penuh M5).
9. **Liputan perancangan kekurangan upaya dan penerusuan perniagaan dalam silibus IFP** — tidak dapat dipastikan sama ada wujud dalam modul IFP setara M2 CFP (PERLU SAHKAN).
